Staying in touch rarely fails for lack of wanting to. It fails at the question “Who, and when?”. Ruf durch (German for “give them a ring”) takes that decision off your hands: sort your contacts by how much they matter to you once, and every day the app suggests exactly one person you could reach out to.

No guilt, no red badges, no to-do list – just one friendly suggestion a day.

HOW IT WORKS
• Bring in your contacts: from your address book (name and phone number only, only when you ask) or add them by hand.
• Sort them: Very important (about weekly), Often (every two weeks), Now and then (monthly), Rarely (every three months) or Never suggest.
• One suggestion a day: people who matter to you and haven’t heard from you in a while are more likely to come up. Someone you just talked to won’t come up again right away.
• One tap is enough: Call opens your phone app with the number, WhatsApp opens the chat. “Not today” is perfectly fine.

HANDY EXTRAS
• Daily notification at your chosen time, on the weekdays you pick – with “Not today”, “Call” and “WhatsApp” right in the notification.
• Coming up: who is likely next over the coming two weeks.
• “Someone else” when today doesn’t suit, and “Log contact” when you talked some other way.
• History with simple stats: contacts in the last 7 and 30 days.
• Pause someone, for example while they are on holiday.
• “Call today” home screen widget in several sizes.
• Wear OS: “Call today” tile and complication with the companion watch app.
• Discreet mode: widget and watch show initials only.
• Learns from your own history: someone who often gets “Not today” comes up less until you are back in touch.

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
• No account, no cloud, no ads, no analytics.
• The app has no internet permission.
• All data is stored encrypted on your device.
• The app doesn’t place calls itself and doesn’t read your call log. It only counts what you tap or log in the app.
• Backup as a password-protected, encrypted file – for a new phone or just in case. Contacts and history can also be exported as a spreadsheet (CSV).

Available in German and English, following your device language.
