Coming soon to Google Play

What the app does

  • Import contacts from your address book (read-only, only if you want) and rank them by importance
  • Daily draw by importance and how long it has been, learning as you go
  • Notification with “Call”, “WhatsApp” and “Not today”
  • “Call today” widget, watch tile and a discreet mode that shows initials only

Your data stays yours

  • No account, no ads, no tracking.
  • Your entries stay encrypted on your device.
  • Backup and export only when you want, as a file you keep yourself.

All details are in the privacy policy.
